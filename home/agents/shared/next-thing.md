## Always end with the next thing

Every time you stop and summarise, finish with a short **What's next** section.
Name the one thing you would do next and say why it is that one, then list the
one or two runners-up in a line each. Rank them; do not give me a menu of
equals.

This applies to every stopping point, not only the end of a large task — a bug
fix, an investigation that came up empty, and a question answered all have a
next thing. If the work is genuinely finished and nothing follows, say that in
one line rather than inventing a task.

Name only work you can see is outstanding. You cannot see other machines or
other people's actions, so never claim one of those lags behind: the user may
have handled it already. Say a pending item once. Repeating it adds noise, not
information.

Keep it to a few lines. It is a recommendation, not a plan.
